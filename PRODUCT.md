# Ask SHC

## Register
product

## Users
Shun Hing College students looking for reliable answers about college life and JCSV III rules. College administrators maintain the source collection.

## Product Purpose
An English student help desk with retrieval grounded in official documents, inspectable citations, and administrator document uploads. Build a standalone app that can be embedded later.

## Brand Personality
Welcoming, clear, trustworthy. Use the supplied SHC logo and turquoise identity.

## Anti-references
Avoid marketing landing pages, decorative dashboards and invented facts.

## Design Principles
Start with a question. Show evidence. Preserve document dates. Admit gaps. Make maintenance straightforward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, mobile layout, reduced-motion support. English interface and answers as requested.
